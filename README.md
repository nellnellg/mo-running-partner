# Mo, your running partner

A prototype of Mo, a shopping agent for running shoes. You tell Mo about a shoe you run in, with three taps. Mo keeps your rotation, speaks up when a shoe is probably due, and helps you choose the next one by comparing it to the shoes you already own.

Mo's picks and reasons come from a real AI model, working from a small catalog of real shoes with list prices from fall 2026. Mo does not connect to Strava or Garmin. It estimates mileage from what you tell it, so it says "probably." Your shoes are saved in your own browser.
