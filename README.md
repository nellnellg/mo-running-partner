# Mo, your running partner

A clickable prototype of Mo, an AI agent for runners. Mo checks your shoe rotation, finds the gaps and recommends shoes that fit your training and budget.

This is a design prototype. The conversation is scripted, and the Strava connection is simulated with a sample runner. Shoes are real models with list prices from September 2026, so check retailers for current prices.
